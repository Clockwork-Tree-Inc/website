What Atlas does

Eighteen things Atlas does

01Proves a real, live person did something

Every action can carry proof that a living human performed it, not a bot, an AI or a recording.

02Makes stolen credentials useless

Someone with all your information still cannot log in as you. Access requires you to be physically present and alive.

03Keeps your data encrypted, even from us

Your files and messages are stored encrypted on your own devices. The company running Atlas cannot read, copy or hand them over.

04Makes a lost or stolen device worthless

A taken device gives a thief no access to your accounts or data, because nothing works without your live presence.

05Proves a photo or video is real and untouched

Media captured through Atlas carries proof a live person took it, at a specific time, and that it has not been altered since.

06Records evidence during an emergency

A silent signal triggers encrypted audio, video and sensor recording, stored where it cannot be deleted or altered.

07Detects when you are being forced

If someone coerces you into unlocking your account, the system protects your data instead of releasing it, and records the coercion.

08Recovers everything without revealing who you are

Restore a lost account by proving in person that you are a live human, without anyone learning your name or which account is yours.

09Verifies a human is behind an AI agent

When an AI agent acts for you online, Atlas can prove a real person authorised it.

10Keeps your messages and calls private, now and later

Messages and calls are sealed end to end with protection built to withstand future quantum computers. Calls ring on your phone's own call screen, you choose how much of your traffic pattern to hide, and Tor is built in.

11Lets you live separate lives online

Personas for work, family and public life that nobody can link to each other or to you. Your passport-verified real ID shows only on the persona you choose.

12Puts you in charge of who reaches you

Contacts come by request, with a first message you accept or decline. Your door decides who gets through and through which trusted spaces, and a block stops the person themselves, not just one account or persona, without you ever learning who they are.

13Signs you in to ordinary websites without passwords

Atlas gives every website its own passkey, so no two sites can recognise you as the same person.

14Runs on your own server

A node at home on your own computer carries your messages and holds your backups, but holds no keys and cannot read anything.

15Lets real people vote and review, once each

Each poll sets who can vote, by region and by level of ID, from a passport alone to an in-person check. Votes can be anonymous or fully verified, and either way each real person counts once.

16Credits the people behind AI work

Work an AI produces carries who authorised it and the sources it drew on, signed, with a built-in permission saying it must not be used for training.

17Gives what you record a public receipt

Anything you anchor goes into a signed public round made by a group of independent nodes. You keep a receipt that anyone can check later to prove when it existed.

18Lets websites check for a real person without learning who

A website can ask whether a verified live human is there. It gets an answer it can check, tied to its own one-time code, and learns nothing about who you are.

Where we are

Project stage

  • Working code: a Python reference core and a Swift core for the phone, held together by 87 groups of shared test vectors
  • Version 1.3.1: the iPhone app, the Atlas node app for Mac and the Ledger app
  • Over 2,100 automated tests across the Python and Swift cores
  • Every signature post-quantum and bound to its stated purpose
  • An Atlas app for the Ledger Flex as the key device
  • Run end to end on two iPhones using their live sensors, through a relay in Switzerland
  • Provisional patent filed April 28, 2026
  • Novel wallet and wearable design, patent pending
